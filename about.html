About Africa Computer Center & PATH

Practical Access to Technology & Hope

 

Who We Are

 

Africa Computer Center & PATH (ACCPath) is a community-focused organization committed to empowering individuals and transforming communities through technology, education, innovation, and digital inclusion.

 

We believe that technology is a powerful tool for creating opportunities, reducing inequality, and building a brighter future for all.

 

Our Mission

 

To empower communities through practical access to technology, quality education, innovation, and digital skills development.

 

Our Vision

 

A digitally inclusive society where every individual has the opportunity, skills, and confidence to participate fully in the digital world.

 

Our Core Values

 
  • Innovation
  • Empowerment
  • Inclusion
  • Integrity
  • Collaboration
  • Community Impact
 

What We Do

 
  • Digital Literacy Training
  • Computer Skills Development
  • Technology Access Programs
  • Youth Innovation Initiatives
  • ICT Education Support
  • Community Capacity Building
 

Why ACCPath Matters

 

Millions of people still face barriers to technology access and digital opportunities. ACCPath works to bridge this gap by creating pathways to learning, innovation, employment, and community development.
